01 · About the Project

Predicting Manhattan congestion before ambulances get stuck.

Standard navigation only looks at traffic right now—but by the time an ambulance is midway through Manhattan, a clear avenue can turn into gridlock. CITYPULSE forecasts road speeds 15 to 60 minutes ahead, predicts PM2.5 air quality, warns of city-wide crises, and routes emergency vehicles to the nearest hospital along real streets using uncertainty-aware A*.

02 · Model Training & Validation

Strict time splits, zero data leakage, honest benchmarks.

All models are trained on 15-minute sensor, weather, and PM2.5 timelines split strictly by time (70% train / 10% validation / 20% test). Free-flow speeds, scalers, and historical averages are fit on training data only, verified with a deliberate leakage test.

LightGBM (+15m to +60m)

Trained on 16 past-only features: speed lags, 1h rolling mean/std, train-only usual speed, weather, and road-neighbour averages.

Avg MAE: 0.08079

PyTorch GRU

Sequence model reading 12 past 15-minute steps (3 hours) per sensor to forecast all 4 future horizons together.

Best Avg MAE: 0.07641

PyTorch Graph + GRU

Combines a road-distance Gaussian adjacency matrix with GRU layers and masked MAE loss to model jam spillover.

Avg MAE: 0.08512

Quantile & Crisis Models

P10/P50/P90 Quantile LightGBM for uncertainty bands, Isotonic calibration, TreeSHAP, plus Crisis LightGBM & Isolation Forest.

80% Cov: 79.7% · PR-AUC: 0.98
03 · Key Components

Six modules working together end-to-end.

01

Traffic Forecasting

Predicts Manhattan road speed ratios at +15, +30, +45, and +60 minutes ahead using shared cleaning and feature code in src/common.py.

02

Uncertainty & SHAP

Quantile LightGBM estimates worst-case slowdowns (P90 travel time), while TreeSHAP explains the top drivers behind every prediction.

03

Crisis & Congestion Index

Computes a live 0–100 Manhattan Congestion Index and warns 30 minutes before >30% of sensors lock up simultaneously.

04

PM2.5 Air Quality

Forecasts PM2.5 at +1h, +3h, and +6h using traffic density, wind, rain, and temperature, mapped to official AQI categories.

05

A* Hospital Dispatch

Click anywhere on Manhattan to run A* with predicted travel times + uncertainty penalties and snap to real streets toward the nearest hospital.

06

FastAPI & Live Weather

Pulls live Central Park (KNYC) weather observations and serves predictions, crisis alerts, and routes over REST endpoints.
